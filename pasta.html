Garlic Butter

Prep Time 10 min
Cook Time 15 min
Servings 2s

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook spaghetti according to package directions. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the minced garlic and chili flakes, and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the drained pasta to the pan, along with a splash of the reserved pasta water. Toss to coat.
  5. Stir in the parmesan and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Top with fresh parsley and serve immediately.
