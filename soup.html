Tomato Basil Soup

Prep Time10 min
Cook Time25 min
Servings4

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 800g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1/4 cup cream (optional)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and vegetable stock. Bring to a simmer.
  4. Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Remove from heat and stir in the fresh basil. Blend until smooth using a blender or immersion blender.
  6. Stir in the cream if using, and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve hot with crusty bread.
